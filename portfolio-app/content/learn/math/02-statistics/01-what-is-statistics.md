Goal: Define statistics, name the main statistical measures, and give real uses of
statistics.
The idea in plain words Statistics is the science concerned with developing and studying
methods for collecting, analysing, interpreting and presenting data. In short, it is a tool
that helps us extract information and knowledge from data.
The main statistical measures
Range: the gap between the largest and the smallest value.
Mean: the average value.
Standard deviation: how far the values spread away from the mean.
Correlation: how two things move together, for example work experience and salary.
Example. Who is the best footballer in the world between 2010 and 2020? Statistics
answers this by collecting every player's results and comparing their average, which is
the mean.
Applications of statistics
Six Sigma. A data driven business technique that improves quality by removing the
causes of defects and reducing variation in a process. Statistics about a company's
products help it work more efficiently.
Weather forecasting. Collect information about the weather and climate at a given
time of the year, then use it to predict the weather.
Role of statistics in machine learning. The path is Data, then Machine Learning models.
Statistics prepares, summarises and checks the data before a model learns from it.
Key rules
Statistics collects, analyses, interprets and presents data.
The core measures are range, mean, standard deviation and correlation.
Practice
Q1. In one sentence, what is statistics?
A1. The science of collecting, analysing, interpreting and presenting data to get
information from it.
Q2. Which measure tells you how spread out the values are around the mean?
A2. The standard deviation.
Q3. What does Six Sigma try to reduce?
A3. Defects and variation in a process.
Where this is used in machine learning: Before any model is trained, statistics is used to
understand the data: its averages, its spread and how its features relate to each other.

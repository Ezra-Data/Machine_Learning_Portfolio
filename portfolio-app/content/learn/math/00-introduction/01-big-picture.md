Goal: Name the four areas of math used in machine learning and say what each one is for.
The idea in plain words Every machine learning system follows one path: Data, then
Model, then Training.
Data is information turned into numbers, such as the size, age and number of
bedrooms of a house.
A model is a rule that turns data into an answer, such as a predicted price.
Training means adjusting the model until its answers are close to the true ones.
Note The build agent shows these three steps as three cards joined by arrows.
Math is the language of every step. Four areas do the work:
Calculus in one minute. There are two types. Differential calculus splits something into
very small pieces and looks at how it changes. Integral calculus adds many small pieces
back into one large whole. Gradient descent, the method that trains most models, is built
from differential calculus. Lesson 4.4 builds it from scratch.
Key rules
Data, then Model, then Training is the path every ML project follows.
Linear algebra stores and combines data. Statistics describes it. Probability measures
uncertainty. Calculus improves the model.
Practice

A2. Probability.
Q3. Which kind of calculus adds many small pieces into one large whole?
A3. Integral calculus.

## Project Overview

Chana is a dedicated campus chatbot and navigation system developed for **Dedan Kimathi University of Technology (DeKUT)**. Built by Ezra and Team WAHENGA during a university hackathon, the application helps students, new visitors, and faculty find campus locations and query administrative and academic information.

- **Ezra's Role**: Team Member & ML Developer

## Problem Statement

Navigating a large university campus and obtaining quick, reliable answers to common location and departmental questions can be challenging for freshmen and visitors. Chana bridges this communication and orientation gap through unified conversational responses and campus navigation guidance.

## Approach & Architecture

- **Conversational Interface**: Guides users to key facilities, labs, lecture halls, and administrative offices through natural language prompts.
- **Campus Route Guidance**: Connects key campus waypoints to assist users in moving between departments efficiently.
- **Reliable Domain Information**: Provides verified answers regarding university services and common student inquiries.
